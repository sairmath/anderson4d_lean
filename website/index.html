Conditional Theorem 1.1

The public capstone

Prop36Family M rho → MainLawStatement M rho

For each fixed noise model and cutoff satisfying the external hypothesis, there is a positive coupling threshold below which every finite family of Fourier modes converges in distribution to the stated centered Gaussian limit. Lean records joint convergence through characteristic functions and derives the finite-vector form.
