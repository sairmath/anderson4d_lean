Conditional Theorem 1.1

The public capstone

Prop36Family M rho → MainLawStatement M rho

For each fixed noise model and cutoff satisfying the external hypothesis, there is a positive coupling threshold below which any finite family of Fourier modes converges in distribution to the stated centered Gaussian limit. Lean records this through joint characteristic functions and derives the usual finite-vector form.
